HOW TO REBUILD ENCLAVE
======================

Whenever you have an updated version of this folder, here is ALL you do:

1. Keep this whole folder together in one place.

2. Double-click:  BUILD INSTALLER.bat

   A window opens and does everything by itself:
     - checks the app is healthy (self-test)
     - builds the app
     - builds the installer
   Then it pops open a folder with your new installer:
     Output\EnclaveSetup-1.7.0.exe

3. That EnclaveSetup file IS your app. Double-click it to install it,
   or send it to someone else to install.

If the window shows a red "FAILED" line, copy what it says and send it
to Claude - it will tell you which step to fix.

Note: the first time you run the installer, Windows may say
"Windows protected your PC". That is normal for a free app. Click
"More info", then "Run anyway".
